--- layout: default permalink: / body_class: proto-grid styles: [michi, lark, kozeni, grid] scripts: [album, lark, kozeni, grid] --- {%- comment -%} The home page: Swiss editorial, the site's 12-column grid made visible and kinetic. The first screen is a poster, the tagline and the lede at its foot and nothing else (the rooms after it introduce the apps, a screen each, and the running index over them leads to each), and it arrives the way every screen after it does. For a beat the screen is the ink (true black in dark mode, so nothing flashes white at night), its grid ruled down it in hairlines, and only the square mark lit, its bit coming out of its notch. Then twelve columns of the canvas (six on a phone) strike up the full height of the screen, left to right, quicker as they go, as Michi's will, each carrying its strip of the tagline, and push the ink out of the top; the header and the lede are uncovered column by column as the columns pass. Then the i's dots, square bits the width of the stem, pop out of their stems one after another, the square full stop slides in along the baseline and clicks onto the right margin, which lights for a moment, and the poster gives a pixel and settles. Last, the redline lens sweeps once across the tagline, and goes. Readable by about two thirds of a second, still by about two seconds; once it's left alone, Michi's ground lifts once in the first column at the foot. With a mouse the lens follows the pointer, and on a touch screen a sideways drag on the tagline scrubs it: through it the poster is in outlines, with the decisions in that strip of it written on (grid.js). Under Reduce Motion, without the script, in forced colors and on paper, the poster is simply there, composed. Then a room for each app, and the Studio with Contact: one screen each. Scrolling snaps a screen at a time (CSS scroll snap; the keys that step a screen and the wheel are taken by grid.js, so they go the same way in every browser, one press or gesture a screen). Each screen arrives as twelve columns of its ground (six on a phone), each carrying its strip of the room's name and number, so the name is built a column at a time and clicks into register as the columns settle; each column pushes the strip of the screen before's big type out ahead of it, like the flaps of a departures board (all but a poster of three or four lines, a phone's or a tablet's, which is covered where it stands: too tall to push, its strips would carry its lines past each other); then the rest of the room is uncovered column by column. Each app's columns keep its own time: Michi's struck home left to right like a postmark, Lark's in a call and its echo, Kozeni's counted in like coins with its spine dropped last into its slot; the Studio's come down as a curtain. The header runs over every screen in that screen's ink, and over the rooms the index, 01 to 03. Opened on a room (a link to it, /#lark or the apps' /#contact, or to a passage in it, a reload on it, a step back to it), the page opens on the void as the poster does, and the room's columns rise over it in its rhythm, carrying its name; the rooms before it are at rest under it. (A step back that the browser answers from its cache shows the page as it was left, on the reader's screen.) How it's built (grid.css says more): every screen after the first is an article, a screen's height of track, then the screen, then a screen's height of tail (none for the last). Each overlaps the two before it, so its screen is held to the viewport from the moment the screen before it comes to rest until the one after it does. grid.js turns that on (g-fx) and draws the columns, on springs that follow the scroll. Without it, under Reduce Motion, in forced colors or on paper, the screens simply stack, edge to edge, and still snap. The header and footer drawn in the screens (.g-head, .g-foot-copy) are copies, aria-hidden and inert; the real ones, from the layout, lie over them, clear, and take the pointer and the keyboard. Addresses outside this site point at ids on this page: App Store Connect's Support URL for every app is https://tinybit.studio/#contact, and an app's Marketing URL, if set, is its room (/#kozeni, /#lark, /#michi), whose id is its key in _data/apps.yml. Keep the id contact and the apps' keys, or those links land at the top. {%- endcomment %} {%- comment -%} Before the first screen is laid out. So nothing of the page under the void, the finished room or the screen the browser happens to paint first, is ever seen before its arrival. The browser's bar takes the void's colour here, before the first frame, so it never goes from the canvas to the void and back; and should the deck never take over (its script not loading), it's put back, as the void goes, after three seconds.
