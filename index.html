Studio

This page is laid out the same way: no rules between sections, only room. Lark’s design notes put it more bluntly: a divider is “an ornament doing the job of the room it takes up.”

Most of the work is like that. Small, exact changes, each described in one plain sentence. On their own they are hard to see. Together they are the app. Hence the name.
