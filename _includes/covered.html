{%- comment -%} Covered: Lark's exhibit. A sentence shown covered, the way Lark covers a line during your turn: one bar per word, punctuation included, so each word keeps its length and no glyph shows. "Show the sentence" uncovers it with its English meaning, then reads "Cover the sentence" (the label names what a press does, so there is no aria-pressed to contradict it). Without JavaScript the sentence and meaning simply show and the button stays hidden. One per page (ids).
