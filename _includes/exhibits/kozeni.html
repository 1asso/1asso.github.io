{%- comment -%} Kozeni's exhibit: its keypad, taking an amount the way Kozeni's does (kozeni.js), on its room's coral (kozeni.css). "New Expense" is the title Kozeni gives the sheet for a new expense; the keys are its keypad's, three across in its order. Kozeni's Add, Category and date keys are left out: nothing here saves. Without JavaScript the keys are a picture (aria-hidden), and the hidden description says so. kozeni.js makes each key a button, named by its data-label or else its text (Delete is also described by "Hold to clear."), puts them in a group named by the keys' own data-label, and takes the description out. The amount is silent to screen readers: the status under it says each new amount a moment after the last key. {%- endcomment -%}
